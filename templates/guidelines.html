NeighborGrid Mohalla Guidelines

Principles for keeping our Indian communities safe, cooperative, and respectful.

Emergency Services Notice (Dial 112 / 100 / 108)

NeighborGrid is NOT an emergency response dispatch service. If someone is in immediate danger, facing severe medical distress, or witnessing fire or violence, call 112 (National Emergency Support) or 108 (Ambulance) immediately.

1. Pure Non-Commercial Mutual Aid

No money, cash demands, buying/selling of products, or business promotions. Neighbors help neighbors freely—whether lending a ladder, helping shift heavy furniture, or offering a metro ride.

2. Privacy & Phone Number Shielding

WhatsApp and phone numbers are kept completely concealed from public posts. Direct contact info is only unlocked between two residents after the post owner explicitly accepts an offer to help.

3. Safe Meeting & Public Spots

For borrowing tools or book exchanges, prefer meeting at society gates, building lobbies, community parks, or nearby tea stalls/metro stations. Let family members know.

4. Zero Tolerance for Spam & Misconduct

Harassment, discriminatory language, caste/religious slurs, and commercial spam will lead to immediate post removal and permanent account banning by moderators.

Community Moderation

See a post violating our mutual-aid code? Click "Report Post" on any listing to notify our Resident Welfare moderators instantly.
