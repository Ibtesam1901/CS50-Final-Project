NeighborGrid Community Guidelines

Principles for keeping our neighborhoods safe, cooperative, and respectful.

Emergency Services Notice

NeighborGrid is NEVER a replacement for 911 / emergency services. If you or anyone around you is in immediate physical danger, experiencing a medical crisis, or witnessing a fire or crime, stop and call emergency dispatch immediately.

1. Non-Commercial Mutual Aid

NeighborGrid is built on community solidarity and mutual assistance. No money, barter demands, commercial selling, or tipping is allowed. Help is offered and accepted freely.

2. Privacy & Consent

Personal phone numbers and direct contact details are kept strictly concealed until a post owner explicitly accepts an offer to help. Never share private neighbor information externally.

3. Safe Meeting Practices

When lending tools, exchanging items, or meeting a neighbor for the first time, meet in well-lit public or semi-public areas (porches, building lobbies, curbside). Let someone know where you are going.

4. Zero Tolerance for Misuse

Harassment, discriminatory language, hate speech, illegal items or substances, and weapon requests are strictly prohibited. Violators will have their posts hidden and accounts permanently revoked.

Flagging & Reporting

If you ever see a post that violates these guidelines or poses safety concerns, click the "Report" button on the post card. Our community moderators review reported items continuously.
